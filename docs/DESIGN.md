## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-trust, institutional analytics platform engineered for academic leaders, provosts, department chairs, and educators. The emotional resonance is calm, methodical, objective, and scholarly. It strips away commercial clutter to emphasize clarity of data, pedagogical rigor, and actionable sentiment trends.

The visual direction combines **Corporate Modern** architecture with precision-driven **Data-Dense SaaS** patterns:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, clear, empathetic, and systematically organized.
- **Structure:** Crisp white and slate container strata engineered for high scannability across multi-layered student survey corpora and longitudinal sentiment charts.
- **Semantic Differentiation:** Feedback sentiment is parsed via strict, unambiguous color cues (Emerald, Amber, Rose) while brand context and global structural controls remain grounded in Deep Navy and Indigo.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional, data-first environment adhering rigorously to WCAG AAA contrast standards against white and slate-50 backgrounds.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Deep Navy. Anchors top navigation, active breadcrumbs, high-emphasis text, primary headers, and structural control toggles.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5`, `#6366F1`):** Electric Indigo. Represents platform intelligence, deep analytics filters, primary call-to-actions, active metric highlights, and focused interactive states.
- **Neutral Core:**
  - Base Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` (White) and `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100 for secondary well containers)
  - Borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) and `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300)
  - Text: High contrast `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for headers; `#334155` (Slate 700) for body copy; `#64748B` (Slate 500) for metadata and labels.

### Functional Sentiment Tiers
- **Positive Sentiment (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Emerald. Signals high satisfaction scores (NPS/CSAT &gt; 80%), commendable faculty feedback, and upward retention velocity.
- **Neutral / Ambient Sentiment (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Amber. Denotes passive responses, mid-tier evaluations, and items needing proactive monitoring.
- **Negative / Alert Sentiment (`#E11D48` / `#EF4444`):** Rose/Crimson. Pinpoints systemic student complaints, low engagement thresholds, course attrition warnings, and safety issues.

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Inter** across all typographic applications to ensure extreme tabular legibility, neutral institutional rendering, and flawless numerical parsing across complex analytic dashboards.

- **Tabular Numerics:** For all metrics (`metric-xl`, `metric-lg`), table views, percentage deltas, and cohort volume tallies, apply the OpenType feature `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`. This preserves column vertical alignment across changing data.
- **Labels & Overlines:** `label-md` and `label-sm` should be used for survey cohort chips, sentiment badges, chart legends, and input micro-copy. When rendered as overlines, they use full uppercase with `0.04em` tracking.
- **Student Quote Quotes / Direct Feedback:** Render actual qualitative student comments in `body-md` using italic variations with Slate 700 to immediately distinguish subjective quotes from quantitative metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, organized as a responsive, fluid 12-column grid within bounded metric workspaces.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Maximum application shell constraint of `1600px` to maintain optimal data glanceability.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` canvas margins. Dual-column analytics cards reflow into stacked modules.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Filters collapse into sliding bottom-sheet drawers, and table columns degrade to vertically stacked summary cards.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Data Densities:** Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled metadata (e.g., metric label to value, tag internal padding).
- **Module Breathing Room:** Dashboard widgets and sentiment breakdown cards use internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) to separate multi-dimensional data charts from tabular lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses an approach of **low-profile ambient elevation coupled with precise outline boundaries**. High-gloss, skeuomorphic, or dramatic blurs are rejected in favor of clinical clarity.

### The Two-Layer Depth Model
1. **Base Surface:** Background rendered at `#F8FAFC`.
2. **Elevated Containers (Cards, Modals, Popovers):** Rendered on pristine `#FFFFFF` surfaces wrapped in a mandatory `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border.

### Shadow Scale
- **Flat/Embedded:** No shadow. Background is `#F1F5F9` with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Used for metric well-boxes and nested code/student text quotes.
- **Level 1 (Default Metric Cards):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);` bordered by `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards, Table Row Hovers):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);` accompanied by an accent-tinted border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Filter Flyouts, Context Popovers):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);` bordered by `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 4 (Drill-down Modals & Feedback Inspector Panels):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);` with a backing scrim of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, modern geometry based on level `2` roundedness, configured purposefully to feel humanized yet institutionally credible.

- **Primary Card Containers & Analytics Modules:** Standardized to `14px` border radius (`rounded-xl` equivalent). This smooths dense data displays while maintaining structural grid discipline.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Dropdown Menus, Buttons):** Configured to `8px` (`rounded-md`) to ensure precise hit areas that fit snugly inside dense filter ribbons.
- **Badges, Tags, and Sentiment Pills:** Fully rounded at `9999px` (`rounded-full`) to immediately signal categorical status, tag attributes, and isolated rating scores.
- **Nested Progress/Sentiment Bars:** Track paths and dynamic sentiment fill indicators use a uniform `6px` radius to prevent visual harshness in data bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) or Brand Indigo (`#4F46E5`), text `#FFFFFF`, height 40px, padding `0 16px`, border-radius 8px. Hover state shifts brightness by +8% with Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover state applies `#F8FAFC` background and `#0F172A` text.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#475569`. Hover applies `#F1F5F9`.

### Sentiment Chips & Badges
- **Structure:** Height 24px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `2px 10px`, font `label-sm`.
- **Positive:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Includes a 6px solid `#10B981` dot on the left.
- **Neutral:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`. Includes a 6px solid `#F59E0B` dot on the left.
- **Negative / Issue:** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#9F1239`, border `1px solid #FECDD3`. Includes a 6px solid `#E11D48` dot on the left.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Box:** Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px, font `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#4F46E5` with an outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Placeholder:** `#94A3B8`.

### Analytics Cards & Survey Summaries
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius 14px, Level 1 elevation.
- **Card Header:** Border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`, internal padding `16px 24px`. Houses the metric label, optional info tooltip, and right-aligned timeframe pills.
- **Card Body:** Internal padding `20px 24px`. Houses primary `metric-xl` figures, comparative delta badges (`+4.2% vs last term`), and underlying data visualization.

### Data Tables & Survey Feed Lists
- **Header Cells:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-sm`, tracking `0.04em`, text `#475569`, border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `12px 16px`.
- **Row Elements:** Border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`, alternating hover background `#F8FAFC`, text `body-md`.
- **Verbatim Feedback Row:** Displays a collapsed 2-line snippet of actual student comments with an inline expandable button and automated sentiment tag.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px box, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, border-radius 4px. Checked state fills with `#4F46E5` displaying a white check icon.
- **Segmented Range Control (Likert Scales 1–5):** Enclosed flex container with `#F1F5F9` background, `4px` padding, and `8px` radius. Active option snaps into elevated `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 elevation and `#0F172A` bold text.
